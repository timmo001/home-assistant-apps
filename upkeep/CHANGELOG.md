## Dashboard

- The Releases link in the menu counts the repositories due a release by what they suggest, with a badge each for major, minor and patch in the same colours as the Releases page: red for major, amber for minor and blue for patch. A badge only shows when its count isn't zero, and hovering one says what it counts.
