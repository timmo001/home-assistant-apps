## Jobs

- **Attention** reads how items tie together on GitHub: stacked pull requests, the issues a pull request closes, parents, sub-issues and blockers.

## Dashboard

- **Groups** on the Attention page: items that belong to one piece of work show together as a card, in the section of their most urgent item. Set a group's focus to Today to pin it at the top, or Parked to fold it away.
- Each item's **Group** menu moves it into a group, or starts a new one. GitHub's links leave your own groups alone.
- A group's menu adds an **outside wait**, such as a release next month, or renames it. Groups you made can be removed.

## Server

- Forms GitHub's groups from each collection, keeping a group's focus, notes and waits as its items change.
