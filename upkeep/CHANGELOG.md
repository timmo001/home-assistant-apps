## Dashboard

- Every section heading now folds, the same way the inbox's days do: a chevron, the heading and a chip with its count, flat on the page. That covers the inbox, the Jobs page and each job's page (which gains a Runs section), runs, repositories, skills, releases and Attention. Each one remembers in this browser whether you left it open.
- Attention's Parked, Hidden, Unreleased changes, Latest releases, Gone and Agent rules panels are flat sections like the rest now. They still start folded and keep whether you opened them, with their descriptions as a line inside.
- The runs filters from 0.47.0 work like the inbox's now: the table's own search field is back, and a **Filters** button beside it opens a sheet of State, Job, Trigger, Repository and Worker filters. Each filter that's on shows as a chip that takes it off.

## Internals

- Updated groundwork to 0.21.0 in the dashboard, for its new `gw-section`, and docs-kit to 0.20.3 in the docs.
