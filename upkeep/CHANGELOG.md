## Jobs

- **Attention** sends each item's labels.

## Dashboard

- The Attention page reads the shared **status labels** on your own items: Needs Decision puts one under Needs you, In Progress keeps it out of the backlog, and Blocked, On Hold or Future puts it there. The backlog is ordered by status, In Progress, Ready and Planned first.
- Items show their status and type labels as chips.
- **Latest releases** of the repositories on the page are folded away at the end.
- What went is kept apart from Since you last looked, under a **Gone** history of the last week.
