## Dashboard

- In the Home Assistant sidebar, the dashboard takes the whole screen: Home Assistant hides its own sidebar and header around it. The menu button at the start of the dashboard's bar shows Home Assistant's sidebar over the page when you need it. On a phone, where that button opens the dashboard's pages, it's the last item in that menu instead. An older Home Assistant that doesn't support this keeps its sidebar as before.
