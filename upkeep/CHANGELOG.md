## Inbox

- A heal's fix whose commits only moved versions, lockfiles or pins is a version bump now, rather than worth reading. The worker reads each commit a heal pushes for the files it touched, the same way the Watch job judges anyone's commits.
- Once you've read or dismissed an entry, what joins it later is placed on its own, so a routine update after one that wasn't folds away again, rather than staying worth reading for good.
