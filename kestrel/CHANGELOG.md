The player's controls no longer wrap into uneven rows, and the Cameras picker on Recordings can be cleared, or used to pick one camera on its own.

## Live and Recordings

- **Controls in one row, or the phone's layout.** Once the controls don't fit in one row, they take the phone's tidy three-row layout, rather than wrapping unevenly between the two. It's worked out from the controls themselves, so it follows whatever each page shows. On Recordings that's below a window about 1400 pixels wide, and on Live about 980 without sound or picture controls. Wider than a phone, those rows keep a phone's width in the middle of the bar.

## Recordings

- **Clear leaves no cameras.** Clear in the Cameras picker, or unticking the last camera, now leaves none picked, rather than putting the last one back.
- **Pick one camera alone.** Hold Ctrl, Alt or Cmd as you click a camera in the Cameras picker, or press Enter on it, to pick just that one and untick the rest.
