Recordings with several cameras is much more like Live now: show one camera alone, tile them to fit the window, and the cameras stay together at speed. You can also skip from event to event, or watch only the events.

## Recordings

- **Cameras stay together.** While one camera waits for its video, the others hold where they are, then all play on together. Before, at 4× or faster, a slower camera fell behind and kept reloading as it was put back in step, falling further behind each time. A camera that never loads only holds the others for 10 seconds.
- **One camera alone.** Double click a camera, or press Enter, to show it alone, and again, Esc or Backspace to go back. Page Up and Page Down pick the previous or next camera. Shown alone, the other cameras stop loading, so it plays the full stream at speeds where several would drop to the sub stream.
- **Smart tiling.** The cameras are sized to fill the window, as on Live, or in fixed layouts, from the new View menu or T. The choice is kept in this browser.
- **Skip to events.** The new Next event button, or E, goes on to the next motion, PIR or alarm on any camera playing, starting up to 20 seconds ahead of it where there's footage then. W goes back.
- **Events only.** Turn it on with its button, or O, and only the events play, each with a little footage either side, skipping everything between. Play starts at the day's first event. It's kept in this browser.
- **A camera just added** shows at once, shimmering on the player and the timeline until its recordings come. The day's recordings also no longer wait for the month's recorded days to load first.

## Fixes

- Turning a source off in Settings takes its cameras off the Recordings page and its address straight away, rather than leaving them ticked.
- A camera whose list of recordings can't be read shows as nothing recorded, rather than stopping the other cameras from showing.
