Kestrel offers to install itself as an app. Installed, it opens in its own window, starts without a connection, and its icon's menu goes straight to Recordings, Events or Sources.

## Installing the app

- Where the browser can install Kestrel, such as Chrome or Edge, a banner offers it.
- Not now, or turning down the browser's prompt, puts the banner away for good in that browser. Install stays in Settings for as long as the browser can install it.
- Browsers only install over HTTPS or on `localhost`. Safari and other browsers that don't offer installs to the page install from their own menu, such as Add to Home Screen.

## Fixes

- The installed app starts offline after its first visit, rather than only after the second.
- The app's shortcut to Sources no longer goes through the old `/recorder` address.
- Kestrel no longer shows in the share sheet, since it had nothing to do with a share.
- A shortcut opens in the app's window when it's already open, rather than another.
