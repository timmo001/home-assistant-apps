A recording that stops on one camera now says so over that camera's picture, and the message goes once video comes again.

## Recordings

- When a camera's video stops coming, such as the server answering 404 for part of a recording, the reason shows over that camera's tile rather than in a bar above the player. With several cameras playing, you can see at once which one it is.
- The message clears by itself once that camera loads video again, or plays another clip. Before, it stayed above the player until you picked something else, even with every camera playing again.
