Reolink cameras now have picture settings on the Live page too, and the Picture and Move setup panels close as soon as you're done with them.

## Sources

- Reolink cameras, on their own or on a Home Hub or recorder, offer hue, brightness, contrast, saturation and sharpness, each where the camera says it has it. A change applies at once and stays on the camera.
- Reset puts a Reolink camera's picture back to the defaults it reports.

## Fixes

- After you moved a slider in the Picture panel, or changed a preset, home or tracking setting in Move's setup, clicking outside didn't close the panel and Esc asked to discard changes that had already been applied. Both panels now close at once.
