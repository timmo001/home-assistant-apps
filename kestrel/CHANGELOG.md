Twitch channels now play, with their sound, and Settings gives stream and page addresses room to show.

## Sources

- Web pages sources now play Twitch channels. Twitch carries its sound inside the stream, which couldn't be copied as it was, so these streams failed to start at all. Kestrel now makes the sound again as AAC and still copies the picture, so they play with sound. YouTube's live streams keep their sound apart from the picture, and still play without it for now.

## Settings

- Address fields, such as a web page's address and a stream source's main, sub and still addresses, now take a row of their own on a camera's card, so a long address shows in full rather than being cut off after a few words.
